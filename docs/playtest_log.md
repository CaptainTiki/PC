## What stretches play time

- Not route length alone: long sightlines (atriums, catwalks, glass) let you thin enemies from range and play fast.
- Short sightlines (cabins, corners, ducts, shafts) force close fights and searching, and play slow.
- deck03 (three chains, three floors, 18 template cabins) was 6:50 on a first clear. Rooms off a corridor are quick to peek and skip; the chains themselves set the time. Estimate ~2 min per chain plus ~1 min per corridor for planning.
